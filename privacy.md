# Tilehouse privacy policy

Tilehouse is a private dashboard used only by its owner in their own home.

With the owner's permission, Tilehouse reads and changes their Google Nest thermostat's settings (temperature, heating mode, humidity and the room it is in) through Google's Smart Device Management API.

This information is used only to show and control the thermostat on the household's own screens. It is kept only on the household's own computer. It is never sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Home app, or in your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/markalloys/markalloys.github.io.
